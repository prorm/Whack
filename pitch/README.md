# CAPABLE pitch deck

Keep `assets/` beside `index.html` and double-click `index.html` (`file://`) to present completely offline with self-hosted Manrope fonts and real product captures, or run `npx serve pitch` from the repository root. Press `P` in the audience deck to open the synced presenter view window (powered by `BroadcastChannel` with an `opener.postMessage` fallback), then click **Start clock** or press `T` as Pranav begins the 5:00 run of show (`0:00–1:00` Pranav on slides 1–4, `1:00–1:40` Swastik on slides 5–6, `1:40–3:20` Prajwal on slides 7–10: Work Compiler, labs, How it works, Tech stack, and `3:20–5:00` Harsh on slides 11–13: Business model, Ecosystem, close). Advance slides and in-slide builds (on slides 1, 4, 6, and 13) with Page Down, Right Arrow, Down Arrow, Space, or left-click, and go back with Page Up, Left Arrow, or Up Arrow; press `F` for fullscreen, `B` (or `Esc`) for blackout, `Home` for slide 1, or type a slide number (`1`–`13`) and press `Enter` to jump directly. Press `0` at any time (for example in Q&A) to jump into the full-bleed backup slides (`B1` Demand & Work Compiler, `B2` CodeLab 8/8 Verdict & Ed25519 Receipt, `B3` Opportunity Exchange & Career GPS), then press `0` again to return.
