# Community knowledge network

The smallest loop is owner reports a problem, contributor records evidence, expert checks citations, learner practices, learner submits a question/correction/example, moderator adds it to a draft, and the contributor reviews another release. Versions retain source attribution and the exact steps used in each released playbook. Pending questions cannot change the lesson. One synthetic teacher and one synthetic learner are demonstrated in the same device.

| Boundary | Design decision |
| --- | --- |
| Problem / Mission / Skill | Confirm requirements with the publisher. Classify educational simulation, supervised volunteering, paid task, apprenticeship or employment. Keep unknown compensation or location blank |
| Trace / Playbook / Lesson | Append source and cited release version. Revocation of a capture withdraws derivatives. A moved or hidden source disables its lesson |
| Attempt / Evidence / Review | Store numeric evidence, unique identifiers and a hash. Review applies to one submission. Preserve local draft, server status and identity limits |
| Contribution / ConsentGrant | Send correction to moderation. A reviewer edits or declines with attribution. Separate capture, derivative, public and employer permissions |

Contributor incentives worth testing include fewer repeated training explanations, named attribution and recognition based on reviewed, reused work. Disagreements can become cited versions or parallel examples with explicit context. Stolen material is hidden pending owner confirmation. Compensated mentoring, public reputation and org onboarding depend on actual partnerships. Views provide no rank or competence signal.

The small Mission Room executable records individual roles and notes, rejects an unknown role and de-duplicates retried event IDs. It reports an absent verifier contribution rather than crediting the group. Realtime cross-user, identity and AI summaries remain design work.
