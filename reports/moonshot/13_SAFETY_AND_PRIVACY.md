# Safety and privacy

Only synthetic materials went to the cloud experiments. ScrollProof retains private media in IndexedDB and offers local preview and deletion. Regulatory obligations and the rights to distribute real recordings require project-specific review before an external pilot.

**Browser environment finding.** The instrumented capture emitted zero POSTs to the app server. The test machine's endpoint security integration posted a synthetic form marker to its own instrumentation endpoint, even after spelling and autocomplete services were disabled on the field. The browser test reports the external hostname and marker class only. Browser and endpoint tooling are outside the app's control. Establish an approved capture environment and handling rules before using workplace material. [Browser evidence](../../moonshot/benchmarks/browser-results.json)

| Threat | Exercised protection | Residual risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In-source instruction or fabricated step | Data-only model prompt. Deterministic playbook guard blocks missing source references, unresolved/unsafe steps and the known wrong cell | Semantic misinformation can still look sourced. Human check required |
| Sensitive text in descriptions, transcript snippets and job-like strings | Scripted email, mobile, government-ID-shaped and payment-handle cases. Protected stock terms kept | Redaction is pattern-dependent and cannot reliably remove names or secrets |
| Sensitive screenshots or documents | Tested deterministic masking on one known synthetic box. Private uploads have local preview and explicit deletion | Generic OCR/image redaction and frame-by-frame video review are unimplemented. Keep private files local |
| Nonconsensual capture/publication | Explicit capture permission; distinct derivative choice; public sharing off; microphone starts by action and is capped; learner preview separated | Ownership claims remain self-attested. Prove permission before external use |
| Wrong reviewer or leakage | Trainer-code enforcement when configured, token-scoped access, hash-bound receipt, revocation tombstone, demo identity status | Same-device demo cannot establish personhood, author credentials or job readiness |
| Copied or contradictory evidence | Numerical checks are separate from method and identity. Media stays private | Copied correct answers can pass numerical checks; no copying or voice-forgery score claimed |
| Fabricated work, compensation or dangerous directions | Inventory-only fixture, task publisher confirmation, educational demo label, closed skills and local scope | Prose filtering is incomplete and scope must remain supervised. Real listings and minors require independent safeguards |
| Translation obligations drift | Protected formula, counts and SKU. Kannada samples marked pending fluent review. No employer decision derived from translation | Semantic and pronunciation review pending |

The permission model should store distinct creator ownership, license scope, attribution, educational derivative consent, visibility and employer access when those surfaces exist. The scoped demo keeps visibility local. Deletion removes local source, recordings and derivative rows and queues withdrawal of sent worksheets. Past independent learning events are a separate device record. No controlled pilot involved children or workers.
