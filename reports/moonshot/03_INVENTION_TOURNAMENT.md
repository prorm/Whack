# Invention tournament

Scores are design judgments, not market measurements. Each item receives 1–5 for urgency and underserved users (U), distinctiveness and defensibility (D), implementation and repo fit (F), available data and affordable operation (C), partner and privacy burden with less burden scoring higher (T), and network/demo value (N). Weights are 25%, 20%, 20%, 15%, 10%, 10%. Ties favor the smaller credible trial.
