# Founder product specification

**Direction.** Build the Experience Compiler for vocational educators and inventory leads in small shops. The starting pain is repeatedly teaching the same stock-reconciliation judgment and having no evidence that a learner can apply it to unfamiliar figures. The initial loop is capture one safe task, correct it beside the source, publish a version, rehearse, grade a variation and review with consent.

**Architecture.** Represent a Problem, ExperienceTrace, Playbook, Lesson, Skill, Attempt, Evidence, Review, Contribution and ConsentGrant as versioned objects. Store source identity, permissions and cited spans. Use one approved playbook as the source for all formats and bind numeric evidence to the source release. Keep validation, retrieval routing, formulas and recall on the device. Use Lite multimodal at authoring time and strong Flash LOW only when key checks fail. Cache media variants. Review built translations against protected terms and a fluent reviewer. Store media privately on a device until there is a consent-based upload layer. Keep a human-gated display and credential identity separate from grading.

**Market and pricing hypothesis.** Test whether a small educator or employer would pay per verified playbook or managed cohort to reduce repeated coaching load. Measure expert minutes, how often learners reuse a playbook, reviewer burden, transaction volume and transfer. Dollar estimates are in the economics report. Competitors already offer grounded learning, workflow capture and job simulations. The pilot compares the specific combination of correction, offline practice and evidence from an unfamiliar task.

| Priority | Work |
| --- | --- |
| MUST BUILD | Controlled pilot with two stock workflows, trained author/reviewer, offline retention and observable transfer. Add durable review and permission records for pilot users |
| SHOULD BUILD | Attach robust clip extraction to reviewed source intervals, add upload resumption with opt-in, improve screen-reader and Android support, and incorporate fluent Kannada review |
| EXPERIMENTAL | Compare transcript/frames/video routes across independent recordings. Test a more flexible source Q&A that abstains and cites exact evidence. Test correction inheritance over multiple contributors |
| POST-HACKATHON | Organization accounts, public moderation, bounded cross-device recovery and a consenting opportunity partner |
| DO NOT BUILD | Per-learner generative videos, automatic occupational certificates, fake opportunities or speculation about a stock discrepancy's cause |

The hackathon MVP is the nine-step synthetic inventory mission, including the cached extraction, human correction, offline practice, and a demo opportunity preview. The first human milestone is whether contributors correct AI faster than writing a guide and whether learners transfer better than from a generic worked example. [Pilot design](PILOT_PROTOCOL.md)
