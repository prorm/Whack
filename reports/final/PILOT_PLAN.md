# Pilot plan: first real-world test (proposed)

**Question:** Do goal-directed intuition reels plus unseen proof tasks make job seekers measurably better at an unfamiliar version of a real task, and do employers use the evidence to decide whom to interview?

## Shape
- **Role:** one role only. Recommended: inventory & stock assistant (lowest safety risk; tasks already exist in the app). Alternative: rooftop solar helper, but only as *supervised apprenticeship consideration* with a partner who runs the in-person safety practical.
- **People:** 30 job seekers from one ITI or training centre, 3 vocational trainers, 3 local employers.
- **Duration:** 4 weeks. Week 0 baseline, weeks 1–3 learning, week 4 assessment and hiring decisions.
- **Devices:** learners' own Android phones, with offline use expected. Check real low-end devices in week 0.

## What each party does
| Who | Commitment |
|---|---|
| Employers | Each defines one skill mission (task, rubric, pass bar) and reviews only consented evidence. They decide on interviews themselves. |
| Trainers | Sign off each rubric. Review a sample of attempts blind to the auto-grade, to measure agreement. Run the in-person practical where the job needs one. |
| Learners | Week-0 unseen task (baseline), ~10 min/day in the goal feed, proof checks when the feed offers them, week-4 unseen task. |

## Measures (pre-registered)
| Metric | How | Bar to continue |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer gain | Week-0 vs week-4 score on *unseen* task variants | Clear gain over a reading-only comparison group, if one is feasible |
| Delayed recall | Proof check again 7 and 21 days after the first pass | Most passers still pass |
| Grader agreement | Trainer blind grade vs code grade on sampled attempts | High agreement; every disagreement reviewed |
| Misconception repair | Share of failed attempts whose named mistake is gone on retake | Most repaired |
| Opt-in to share | % of passers who share with an employer | Most, without pressure |
| Interview conversion | Interviews per shared candidate, and employer reasons | Employers cite the evidence |
| Placements | Hires within 30 days, retention at 90 days | Reported as-is, small n |
| Reviewer time | Minutes per candidate for trainer and employer | Lower than the current screening |
| Drop-off | Where learners stop; exit interviews | Fix the top 3 causes |

**North-star metric:** learners who get meaningful work after demonstrating new skills. Not daily actives, reels watched or certificates issued.

## Before the pilot starts
- Fluent human review of every learner-facing string in the pilot language (Kannada first). Until then, no language claims.
- Replace DEMO employers, candidates and the DEMO trainer seat with real accounts and real trainer codes (`SCROLLPROOF_TRAINER_CODE`).
- Identity: decide on in-person ID checks at the practical. The app does not verify identity.
- Consent and data: written consent, a data-retention limit, and a revocation route for each learner.
- Safety: hazardous roles only via a partner's supervised practical; no unsupervised work arising from an app pass.

## Cost hypotheses to test (not validated)
Employers' willingness to pay per successful hire (₹1,500–3,000) or a subscription (₹1,499–4,999/month); institutions per learner (₹100–300/month). Ask after evidence exists, not before.
