# CAPABLE: learn by doing, remember by scrolling, get hired through proof

CAPABLE is an experiential upskilling platform. It turns practical knowledge into demonstrable skills, and demonstrable skills into opportunities. FLUX is the learning and verification engine underneath.

## The product hierarchy (do not blur it)

| Experience | Job | Never does |
|---|---|---|
| **FLUX Learn** (primary) | Builds understanding through narrated, interactive lessons and a project | Award credentials |
| **FLUX Reels** (Revise) | Retention: short reels for concepts already learned, spaced 1 · 3 · 7 · 21 days | Teach new material, award anything |
| **Proof Passport** | Evidence from unseen tasks and projects, graded by code, inspectable | Count watch time or reel answers |
| **Opportunities** | Explainable matching, consent-scoped sharing, supervised next steps | Hide a score, imply a job offer |
| **Field Scars** | Experienced workers turn real mistakes into reviewed cards | Publish without the sieve and 2 master reviews |

## FLUX Learn: how one lesson works
The lesson is a cinematic stage you take part in (house style: deep navy, mood tint, perspective grid, glass panels, rolling mono numbers, karaoke captions, Chirp3-HD narration over a music bed):

1. **Curiosity:** a cold open (06:47, an inverter dies).
2. **Teach:** narrated scenes, from absolute basics (electricity as water).
3. **Predict:** you commit before being told. Wrong guesses are named, not just marked wrong.
4. **Experiment:** live sliders with a goal ("drag until 12 panels cross 600 V"). The scene responds instantly.
5. **Explain:** the rule appears as a line-by-line trace with tagged steps (GIVEN, COLDER, ROUND DOWN).
6. **Test:** checks with hints. After two misses you can ask for the answer, and the miss is recorded.
7. **Train:** fresh, seeded problems (new panel, city and numbers every set).
8. **Apply:** lesson 5 is a real job ticket, scaffolded step by step.
9. **Project:** design a 14-panel roof against six live checks and explain it to the customer. It goes on the Proof Passport.
10. **Prove alone:** an unseen work order, then the job match.

The first course is *Rooftop Solar Technician* (5 lessons + project, about 35 minutes). EV charging and payments exist as single story episodes.
